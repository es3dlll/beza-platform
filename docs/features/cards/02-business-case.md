# Cards Business Case

## Revenue Model
| Revenue Stream | Calculation Model | Margin | Y3 Annual Revenue |
|---------------|-------------------|--------|-------------------|
| Virtual Card Issuance Fee | 5,000 SYP (one-time) | 90% | $1.5M |
| Physical Card Issuance Fee | 15,000 SYP (one-time) | 70% | $3M |
| Card Replacement Fee | 10,000 SYP (lost/damaged) | 80% | $500K |
| Interchange Income (Online) | 1.2% per domestic / 1.8% intl txn | 85% | $2.5M |
| Interchange Income (POS) | 0.8% per txn | 85% | $1.5M |
| ATM Withdrawal Fee | 2,000 SYP + 0.5% of amount | 90% | $1M |
| Monthly Card Maintenance | 2,000 SYP/month (optional premium) | 95% | $2M |
| FX Spread (USD transactions) | 2% on international transactions | 100% | $1M |
| One-time Card Fee | 2,000 SYP per single-use card | 90% | $500K |
