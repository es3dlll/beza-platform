# Cards Market Analysis

## Competitive Landscape (Syria 2026)

### Direct Competitors
| Competitor | Type | Market Share | Key Weakness |
|------------|------|-------------|--------------|
| Local Bank Debit Cards | Traditional debit | 5% of population | Bank account required, low acceptance |
| Syriatel Cash Prepaid | Telecom prepaid | 2% | Limited to telecom ecosystem, no international use |
| MTN MoMo Prepaid | Telecom prepaid | 1% | Same limitations as Syriatel |
| Cash (Physical) | Default payment | 95%+ daily txns | Cannot pay online, unsafe to carry |
| Hawala (Remittance Cards) | Informal | 3% of diaspora | Slow, expensive, unregulated |

### Regional/International Competitors (accessible from Syria)
| Competitor | Type | Threat Level |
|------------|------|-------------|
| AdvCash | E-wallet + card | Medium — used by freelancers, limited local acceptance |
| Payoneer | Freelancer card | Medium — expensive, not consumer-focused |
| Crypto cards (Binance, etc.) | Crypto prepaid | Low — limited adoption, regulatory risk |
| Virtual cards from UAE banks | International cards | Low — very hard for Syrians to open |

## Competitive Advantages
| Beza Advantage | Competitor Gap |
|---------------|---------------|
| Instant virtual card (under 30 seconds) | Banks take 3-7 days for card issuance |
| Apple Pay / Google Pay support | No Syrian-issued card supports digital wallets |
| Per-category spending limits | No competitor offers granular limit controls |
| One-time use virtual cards | No competitor has privacy.com model |
| Freeze/unfreeze from app | Bank cards require branch visit to block |
| Arabic-first interface | All card apps are English-only |
| Real-time spend notifications | Banks provide daily SMS summaries only |
| Local + international BIN | Most cards work only domestically or only internationally |
