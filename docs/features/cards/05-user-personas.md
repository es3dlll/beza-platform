# Cards User Personas

## Persona 1: Layla — Online Shopper
```
Age: 24
Occupation: Marketing coordinator in Damascus
Income: 400,000 SYP/month
Tech literacy: High
Smartphone: Yes (iPhone 14)
Banked: Yes (BBS account)
Pain points:
  - Cannot buy from international e-commerce (Amazon, AliExpress, Shein)
  - Syrian bank cards rejected by most international merchants
  - Must ask friends abroad to buy things for her
  - Wants Apple Pay for contactless in local stores
Needs:
  - International virtual card for online shopping
  - Apple Pay support
  - Per-transaction spending control
  - Real-time notifications for every purchase
  - One-time card for suspicious merchants
```

## Persona 2: Mahmoud — Freelancer
```
Age: 31
Occupation: Freelance software developer (upwork/freelancer)
Income: $1,500/month (USD)
Tech literacy: High
Smartphone: Yes (Samsung Galaxy S23)
Banked: Yes (Syrian bank + Payoneer)
Pain points:
  - Payoneer card fees are high (3.5% withdrawal)
  - Cannot use Syrian card for subscription services (AWS, GitHub, Netflix)
  - Needs USD-denominated card for international payments
  - Current cards have no spending analytics
Needs:
  - USD virtual card for subscriptions
  - Google Pay for in-store
  - Spending category breakdown
  - Higher limits for business tools
  - Multi-currency support (SYP + USD)
```

## Persona 3: Abu Hassan — Business Traveler
```
Age: 45
Occupation: Importer (travels between Syria, Lebanon, UAE)
Income: 3,000,000 SYP/month
Tech literacy: Medium
Smartphone: Yes (iPhone 12)
Banked: No
Pain points:
  - Carries large cash amounts across borders (risky)
  - Hotels require credit card for booking
  - Cannot rent cars without card
  - ATM withdrawals need bank account
Needs:
  - Physical card for POS + ATM
  - International acceptance (Mastercard/Visa)

  - PIN for ATM withdrawals

```
