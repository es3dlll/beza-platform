# Problem Statement

## The Humanitarian Context — Syria

After 15 years of conflict, Syria's humanitarian crisis remains acute:

| Indicator | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| People in need | 13.4M |
| IDPs | 6.8M |
| Refugees hosted in neighbouring countries | 5.4M |
| Population below poverty line | 90% |
| Food insecure | 12.1M |
| Children out of school | 2.4M |
| Damaged/destroyed health facilities | 50%+ |

## Current Distribution Challenges

### 1. In-Kind Aid Inefficiencies
- **Logistics:** Transporting food, blankets, and supplies across frontlines is dangerous and expensive
- **Leakage:** Up to 30% of in-kind aid is lost to theft, diversion, or spoilage
- **Dignity:** Beneficiaries cannot choose what they need; one-size-fits-all hampers

### 2. Cash Distribution Barriers
- **Banking exclusion:** < 20% of Syrians have bank accounts
- **Currency collapse:** Syrian Pound lost 99% of value since 2011; USD-dependent economy
- **Sanctions complexity:** Financial transfers into Syria require OFAC / EU sanctions compliance
- **ID verification:** Many IDPs lack government-issued IDs; UNHCR registration is fragmented

### 3. Donor Reporting Gaps
- No real-time visibility into how cash assistance is spent
- Manual reconciliation processes take 30-90 days
- Outcome measurement (e.g., food security improvement) is anecdotal rather than data-driven

## Why Digital Cash on Beza?

| Problem | Beza Solution |
|---------|---------------|
| Banking exclusion | Mobile wallet on any feature phone (USSD) or smartphone |
| Currency collapse | USD-pegged digital wallet; disbursements in USD-equivalent |
| Sanctions complexity | Automated OFAC/UN/EU sanctions screening at enrolment |
| ID verification | Biometric verification (fingerprint + face) at agent point |
| Leakage | Beneficiary-wallet direct transfer — no intermediaries |
| Donor reporting | Real-time spending analytics with category tracking |
