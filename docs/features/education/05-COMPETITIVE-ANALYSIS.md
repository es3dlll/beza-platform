# 05 — Competitive Analysis

## 5.1 Direct Competitors

| Competitor | Type | Presence | Strength | Weakness |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Syriateq School Manager** | On-prem ERP | 50+ private schools | Mature product, offline mode | No mobile payment, no parent app |
| **Edusys (Biladi Tech)** | Cloud SIS | 30 schools | Good attendance tracking | No payment module, no Arabic UX |
| **Sakhr School System** | Full ERP | 25 schools | Microsoft Partner, MS Schools | Expensive ($10K+/yr), complex |
| **Cash payment at school** | Traditional | 100 % of schools | Incumbent, no tech friction | Parents hate it. High operational cost. |
| **Bank transfers** | Banks | 15 % adoption | Regulated, trusted | Slow (T+1), no school dashboard |
| **Manara Platform** | Fintech | Emerging | Modern UX | Only Damascus, no education vertical |

## 5.2 Indirect Competitors

| Product | Threat Level | Why |
|---|---|---|
| WhatsApp groups + Google Sheets | High for small schools | Free, familiar; but zero reconciliation |
| Paper-based ledgers | Medium | Still dominant in public schools |
| Bank payment kiosks | Low | Only in Damascus/Aleppo, limited |

## 5.3 Beza Differentiation

| Dimension | Competitors | Beza Education |
|---|---|---|
| **Payment** | Cash/cheque/bank transfer only | Wallet, card, instalments, auto-debit |
| **Parent** | No parent app | Full self-serve parent experience |
| **Diaspora** | ✗ | Remittance → school fees in 1 flow |
| **Financing** | ✗ | Buy now, pay later for fees |
| **Real-time** | Batch (end of day) | Real-time reconciliation |
| **Invoicing** | Manual | Auto-generate, termly/monthly |
| **Reminders** | Manual WhatsApp/phone | Automated multi-channel |
| **Integration** | Closed | API-first, ERP integration |
| **Cost** | $5K-$15K/yr + setup | Freemium for schools (2.5% per transaction); Tiny schools free up to 100 students |

## 5.4 Porter's Five Forces

| Force | Assessment | Implication |
|---|---|---|
| Rivalry | Moderate — fragmented, no dominant player | First-mover advantage in fintech+education |
| New entrants | Low barrier in software, high in fintech (licensing) | Regulatory moat via Beza's CBL licence |
| Substitutes | High — paper/WhatsApp | Need amazing UX + zero friction to switch |
| Supplier power | Low — schools have no leverage on platforms | Beza sets terms |
| Buyer power | Medium — large universities could build in-house | Lock in via integrations, financing |
