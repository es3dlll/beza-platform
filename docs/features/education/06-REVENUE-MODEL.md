# 06 — Revenue Model

## 6.1 Revenue Streams

| Stream | Rate | Trigger | Projected Year-1 |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Transaction fee** | 2.5 % of fee value | Every successful fee payment | 1.25 B SYP |
| **School SaaS (Pro)** | 500,000 SYP/month | Monthly subscription | 600 M SYP (100 schools) |
| **Financing interest** | 8-12 % flat | Instalment plans | 300 M SYP |
| **Value-added services** | TBD | SMS credits, API access, analytics | 150 M SYP |
| **FX spread** | 1-2 % above CB rate | Diaspora fee payments | 100 M SYP |
| **Total** | | | **~2.4 B SYP** |

## 6.2 School Pricing Tiers

| Tier | Fee | Max Students | Features |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Free** | 0 | 100 | Basic payment collection, manual receipts |
| **Starter** | 200,000 SYP/month | 500 | Dashboard, SMS reminders, CSV export |
| **Pro** | 500,000 SYP/month | 2,000 | Auto-invoicing, bulk comms, API, multi-faculty |
| **Enterprise** | 1,000,000 SYP/month | Unlimited | Dedicated account manager, custom ERP integration, SLA |

## 6.4 Financing Revenue

- Parent finances 4.5 M SYP at 8 % flat over 12 months
- Total interest: 360,000 SYP
- Beza splits with financing partner (bank) on agreed ratio
