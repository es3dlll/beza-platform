# User Personas

## Persona 1: Layla — The Daily User
**Age**: 28
**Occupation**: Retail store employee, Damascus
**Tech Comfort**: Medium — uses WhatsApp, Facebook, Beza
**Income**: 350,000 SYP/month

**Needs**:
- Top up her mobile (Syriatel) every 3–4 days
- Buy internet data bundles for her home ISP
- Send money/gifts to family in other cities

**Pain Points**:
- Running out of balance mid-call
- Forgetting USSD codes for data bundles
- No easy way to gift credit to her mother in Aleppo

**How Beza Marketplace helps**:
- One-tap Syriatel top-up with saved templates
- Auto top-up when balance drops below 500 SYP
- Gift card purchase sent directly to recipient's Beza wallet
