# Feature: Marketplace

## Overview

Beza Marketplace is a curated digital marketplace embedded within the Beza super-app. It enables users to discover, purchase, and receive a wide range of digital goods and services directly from their Beza wallet. The marketplace operates on a commission-based model, partnering with local and regional vendors to offer competitive pricing while generating revenue on each transaction.

## Core Value Proposition

- **Convenience**: One-stop shop for mobile top-up, internet packages, gift cards, digital goods, and bill payments
- **Speed**: Instant digital delivery for most products via automated fulfillment pipelines
- **Rewards**: Users earn loyalty points on every marketplace purchase
- **Trust**: Curated vendor network with fraud protection and dispute resolution
- **Local-first**: Deep integration with Syrian telecoms (Syriatel, MTN) and regional merchants

## Key Metrics

| Metric | Target |
|---|---|
| Monthly Active Buyers | 50,000 (Y1) |
| Average Order Value | 15,000 SYP |
| Marketplace GMV | 750M SYP/month (Y1) |
| Commission Revenue | 8–15% per transaction |
| Digital Delivery Success Rate | > 99.5% |
| Physical Goods On-Time Delivery | > 95% |

## Product Categories

1. **Mobile Top-Up** — Direct airtime and data bundle purchases via Syriatel/MTN APIs
2. **Internet Packages** — Fixed broadband and fiber data plans from local ISPs
3. **Gift Cards** — Digital gift cards for local and regional merchants
4. **Digital Goods** — Software licenses, game credits, e-books, streaming subscriptions
5. **Bill Payment** — Utility, telecom, and service bill settlement with cashback rewards
