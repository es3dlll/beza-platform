# Government Collections User Personas

## Persona 1: أحمد — The Taxpayer
- **Age**: 42
- **Occupation**: Small shop owner in Damascus (electronics)
- **Tech Comfort**: Medium — uses WhatsApp, Beza for bills, but not advanced features
- **Pain Points**: Property tax requires 2–3 trips to the tax office yearly. Queues are 2+ hours. Paper receipts get lost.
- **Needs**: Pay income and property tax from his phone. Get a digital receipt he can show authorities. Payment confirmation to landlord.
- **Behaviour**: Plans to pay only when reminded. Forgets deadlines. Penalty-averse.
- **Arabic Quote**: "بدي ادفع الضريبة من البيت وما بدي وقف عالدوار من الصبح للظهر."
- **Key Scenarios**: Tax query → pay → receipt; Late payment with penalty calculation

## Persona 2: ليلى — The Student
- **Age**: 20
- **Occupation**: University student, Damascus University (Engineering)
- **Tech Comfort**: High — daily app user, Instagram, Beza for wallet
- **Pain Points**: Semester tuition requires bank transfer with reference number — always gets it wrong. Online portal crashes on deadline day.
- **Needs**: Simple tuition payment with university confirmation. Father transfers money, she pays the university. Receive payment confirmation.
- **Behaviour**: Deadlines-driven. Will pay last minute. Shares payment confirmation on family WhatsApp.
- **Arabic Quote**: "خلصت مصاري التسجيل! لسا بدي ادفع قبل ما يسكر البوابة."
- **Key Scenarios**: Tuition payment by university ID; Payment from parent's Beza wallet

## Persona 3: أبو سامر — The Government Employee
- **Age**: 55
- **Occupation**: Retired civil servant, lives in Homs suburb
- **Tech Comfort**: Low — basic phone, son helps with anything digital
- **Pain Points**: Travels 45 minutes to nearest civil registry office. Cannot read English. Fears being cheated by agents.
- **Needs**: Someone (agent/son) to help him pay passport renewal fee. Get a real receipt. Trust that his money reached the government.
- **Behaviour**: Asks his son Samer to handle all digital payments. Will pay 1–2% extra for agent assistance.
- **Arabic Quote**: "ابني سامر بيدفعلي عالتلفون. أنا بدي وصل."
- **Key Scenarios**: Agent-assisted passport payment; Receipt verification via SMS

## Persona 4: سامر — The Diaspora Son
- **Age**: 31
- **Occupation**: Software engineer in Berlin
- **Tech Comfort**: Very high
- **Pain Points**: Needs to renew Syrian passport for his German-resident children. Embassy requires proof of fee payment in Syria. No family member available to queue.
- **Needs**: Complete passport fee payment from Germany. Get downloadable official receipt. Receipt accepted by Syrian embassy in Berlin.
- **Behaviour**: Willing to pay premium fees. Expects email + SMS confirmation. May pay 200 EUR in fees for convenience.
- **Arabic Quote**: "خلصنا من السفارة. بدي ادفع جوازات سفر لولادي من هون."
- **Key Scenarios**: Diaspora passport fee; Multiple applications in one transaction

## Persona 5: نادية — The Municipal Payer
- **Age**: 38
- **Occupation**: Primary school teacher
- **Tech Comfort**: Medium
- **Pain Points**: Municipality waste collection fee and services tax require separate payments at separate counters. Lost half a day.
- **Needs**: Single view of all municipality obligations. One payment covers all. Automatic yearly renewal.
- **Behaviour**: Prefers auto-pay if available. Needs notification before auto-pay runs.
- **Arabic Quote**: "أربع ساعات بالبلدية عشان أدفع ٢٥ ألف. حرام."
- **Key Scenarios**: Municipality fees combined payment; Auto-pay setup

## Persona 6: وائل — The Fleet Manager
- **Age**: 45
- **Occupation**: Logistics company owner (10 trucks)
- **Tech Comfort**: Medium-High
- **Pain Points**: Vehicle registration for 10 trucks means 10 separate payments. Needs accounting reports. Missed renewal = fines.
- **Needs**: Bulk vehicle registration payment. Exportable payment report for accountant. Reminders 30/14/7 days before expiry.
- **Behaviour**: Will pay for premium features. Expects dedicated account management.
- **Arabic Quote**: "عندي ١٠ شاحنات بدي جدد الكل بضغطة زر."
- **Key Scenarios**: Bulk vehicle registration; Fleet management dashboard
