# Merchant Acquiring Market Analysis

## Competitive Landscape (Syria 2026)

### Direct Competitors
| Competitor | Type | Market Share | Key Weakness |
|------------|------|-------------|--------------|
| Cash (informal) | Physical | 95%+ SME payments | Unsafe, untraceable, limits growth |
| Bank POS terminals | Terminal | 3% of retail | $50-100/mo fees, bank account required |
| Syriatel Cash Merchant | QR + USSD | <1% | Telecom-locked, no POS, no web checkout |
| MTN MoMo Merchant | QR + USSD | <1% | Same as Syriatel |
| International PSPs (Stripe, PayTabs) | Online | <0.5% | Cannot serve offline merchants, USD focus |

### Competitive Advantages
| Beza Advantage | Competitor Gap |
|---------------|---------------|
| Zero monthly fees — only MDR | All incumbents charge monthly rental for POS |
| QR + POS + Link + Web in one product | No competitor offers all four products |
| No bank account required | POS banks require bank account + 6 months history |
| Arabic-first, low-literacy friendly | International PSPs are English-first, complex |
| Works on any smartphone | Competitors require dedicated hardware or specific telco |
| WhatsApp payment links | Unique to Syrian market — meets merchants where they are |
| Same-day settlement to wallet | Banks settle T+2 or T+3 |
| No minimum volume commitment | POS providers require 50+ transactions/month minimum |
