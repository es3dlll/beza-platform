# تعريفات المنتجات — Sharia Product Definitions

## 1. Qard Hasan (قرض حسن)

### Definition
A **benevolent loan** extended by Beza to the user with zero profit/interest. The borrower is obligated to repay only the principal amount.

### Sharia Basis
- Based on the Quranic principle of "قرضاً حسناً" (Quran 64:17)
- Beza may charge **admin fee** (رسوم إدارية) only — actual cost of service, not profit
- Late payment penalty goes to **charity (صدقة)** , not Beza revenue

### Terms
| Parameter | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| Min amount | SYP 50,000 |
| Max amount | SYP 500,000 |
| Min term | 30 days |
| Max term | 180 days |
| Admin fee | 1% of principal (one-time, capped at SYP 5,000) |
| Late fee | SYP 5,000/day → charity |
| Guarantee | 1 friend/family guarantor with active Beza wallet |
| Approval time | ≤ 2 hours |

### Use Cases
- Emergency medical expenses
- School fees (short-term bridge)
- Utility bill gaps
- Small business cash-flow shortfall

## 2. Murabaha (مرابحة)

### Definition
**Cost-plus financing** where Beza purchases a specified good (appliance, electronics, education course) and sells it to the user at cost + disclosed profit margin. The user pays in fixed installments.

### Sharia Basis
- Based on Quran 2:275 ("أحل الله البيع وحرم الربا")
- Two distinct contracts: (1) Beza buys from supplier, (2) Beza sells to customer at markup
- Beza bears ownership risk between purchase and sale
- Profit margin must be **disclosed and fixed** — no compounding or variable rate

### Terms
| Parameter | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| Min amount | SYP 200,000 |
| Max amount | SYP 5,000,000 |
| Min term | 90 days |
| Max term | 24 months |
| Profit rate | 5%–12% flat (depends on term and risk tier) |
| Late fee | SYP 10,000/day → charity |
| Down payment | 10%–20% (user may contribute) |
| Ownership model | Beza owns title → transfers to user after full payment |

### Eligible Purchases
- Home appliances (refrigerator, washing machine, AC, stove)
- Electronics (mobile phone, laptop, tablet)
- Education fees (tuition, vocational training, university)
- Medical equipment
- Furniture

## 3. Micro-Enterprise Financing (تمويل المنشآت الصغيرة)

### Definition
Financing for **small businesses and micro-entrepreneurs** for inventory, equipment, or working capital. Repayment is based on **cash flow analysis** and may follow a flexible schedule tied to business revenue.

### Sharia Basis
- Structured as **Murabaha** for asset purchases (inventory, equipment)
- Or **Qard Hasan** for working capital (with guarantee)
- May also use **Mudaraba** structure in future (profit-sharing)

### Terms
| Parameter | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| Min amount | SYP 500,000 |
| Max amount | SYP 10,000,000 |
| Min term | 90 days |
| Max term | 12 months |
| Admin/Profit rate | 0% (Qard) or 7%–15% flat (Murabaha) |
| Late fee | SYP 15,000/day → charity |
| Cash flow review | Quarterly |
| Collateral | Personal guarantee + business asset pledge |

### Eligibility
- Active Beza Agent account (merchant) ≥ 6 months
- Minimum 20 wallet transactions/month
- Positive cash flow demonstrated via wallet history
- Valid commercial registry (or equivalent informal business proof)

## Late Fee to Charity Mechanism
All late payment penalties across all products are:
1. Tracked in a **separate charity liability account** (not Beza income)
2. Disbursed quarterly to registered charitable organizations
3. Fully documented for Sharia audit
4. Borrowers receive annual statement of charity contributions made on their behalf
