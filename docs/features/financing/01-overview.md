# نظرة عامة — Product Overview & Vision

## Vision
To provide every Syrian individual and micro-entrepreneur with **fair, transparent, and Sharia-compliant financing** that empowers economic participation without riba (interest).

## Mission
Beza Financing bridges the gap between traditional Islamic finance and modern fintech by leveraging alternative credit scoring (wallet transaction history, agent network activity, savings behavior) to extend credit to the unbanked and underbanked in Syria.

## Strategic Objectives
1. **Financial Inclusion** — Serve the 70%+ of Syrians without bank accounts or credit history
2. **Sharia Leadership** — Set the gold standard for Islamic digital financing in the Levant
3. **Risk-Adjusted Returns** — Maintain NPL (non-performing loan) ratio below 3% via ML-based scoring
4. **Speed** — Application-to-disbursement in under 24 hours (Qard Hasan: 2 hours)

## Target Market
- **Primary**: Syrian nationals aged 18–65 with active Beza wallet ≥ 3 months
- **Secondary**: Syrian diaspora sending funds to family (guarantor model for Qard Hasan)
- **Segments**: Individuals (Qard Hasan), Households (Murabaha for appliances/education), Micro-entrepreneurs (Micro-Enterprise)

## Key Metrics (Year 1 Targets)
| Metric | Target |
|--------|--------|
| Active financing users | 50,000 |
| Total disbursed volume | SYP 5 billion |
| Average ticket (Qard Hasan) | SYP 200,000 |
| Average ticket (Murabaha) | SYP 1,500,000 |
| Average ticket (Micro) | SYP 3,000,000 |
| Default rate | < 3% |
| Application approval rate | 45–55% |
| Time to disbursement (Qard Hasan) | ≤ 2 hours |
| Time to disbursement (Murabaha) | ≤ 24 hours |

## Guiding Principles
1. **No Riba** — Zero interest on Qard Hasan; fixed disclosed profit on Murabaha
2. **No Gharar** — Full disclosure of cost, profit, and terms upfront
3. **No Zulm** — Late fees directed to charity, not as penalty revenue
4. **Risk Sharing** — Beza bears ownership risk in Murabaha before sale to customer
5. **Data Privacy** — Credit scoring uses opt-in wallet data with explicit consent
