# Savings User Personas

## Persona 1: Ahmad — The Disciplined Saver (Primary)
- **Age**: 28
- **Occupation**: Software engineer, Damascus
- **Income**: 750,000 SYP/month
- **Savings pattern**: Already saves manually but inconsistently
- **Goal**: New laptop for work (2,500,000 SYP)
- **Pain points**: Forgets to save some months, no way to track progress
- **Needs**: Automated daily savings, clear progress view, profit on savings
- **Tech**: Smartphone, app user, tech-savvy

## Persona 2: Fatima — The Family Organizer (Primary)
- **Age**: 35
- **Occupation**: Teacher, Aleppo
- **Income**: 350,000 SYP/month
- **Savings pattern**: Runs the household jameeya (informal group saving)
- **Goal**: School fees for two children (1,000,000 SYP)
- **Pain points**: Tracking who paid in the jameeya is stressful, defaults happen
- **Needs**: Team goals with member contribution tracking, automated reminders
- **Tech**: Basic smartphone, USDS + app user

## Persona 3: Hassan — The Young Hustler (Secondary)
- **Age**: 19
- **Occupation**: University student + part-time delivery rider
- **Income**: 150,000–200,000 SYP/month
- **Savings pattern**: Spends almost everything, wants to build discipline
- **Goal**: Buy a motorbike (3,000,000 SYP)
- **Pain points**: No savings habit, tempted to spend spare cash
- **Needs**: Round-up feature (painless saving), low minimum deposits, fun milestones
- **Tech**: Smartphone, heavy app user, social

## Persona 4: Umm Khaled — The Jameeya Organizer (Tertiary)
- **Age**: 45
- **Occupation**: Homemaker, rural Idlib
- **Income**: Household 250,000 SYP/month
- **Savings pattern**: Runs an informal jameeya with 10 neighbors
- **Goal**: Home renovation fund
- **Pain points**: Cash collection is unsafe, one member defaulted last cycle
- **Needs**: Digital jameeya with invitation codes, locked contributions, default protection
- **Tech**: USSD + feature phone, reads SMS, has app but limited data

## Persona 5: Layla — The Diaspora Supporter
- **Age**: 32
- **Occupation**: Nurse in Germany
- **Income**: €2,500/month
- **Savings pattern**: Sends money home monthly to family
- **Goal**: Build a family emergency fund in SYP
- **Pain points**: No good savings vehicle for family back home
- **Needs**: Family goal where she contributes from abroad, family withdraws locally
- **Tech**: Smartphone, international app user
