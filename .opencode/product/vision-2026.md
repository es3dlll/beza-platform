# Beza — Financial Operating System for Syria (2026+)

## Platform Vision
Beza is Syria's first Financial Operating System — a unified digital infrastructure that replaces cash with programmable money, connects 18M citizens and 6M diaspora through a single platform, and enables the next generation of financial services across the nation.

## Core Identity
| Attribute | Answer |
|-----------|--------|
| What is Beza? | Financial Operating System |
| What does it replace? | Cash (90%+ economy), informal hawala, fragmented billers |
| Who does it serve? | Citizens, diaspora, merchants, agents, government, NGOs, enterprises |
| What currency? | SYP + USD (multi-currency ready) |
| What channels? | Mobile App (Flutter), USSD (*123#), SMS, Web, Agent POS |
| What is the business model? | Transaction fees, FX spread, MDR, SaaS, lending income |
| What is the scale target? | 5M+ users, 10K+ agents, $2B+ annual TPYear 5 |

## Platform Pillars
1. **Wallet Infrastructure** — Multi-currency programmable wallets
2. **FX Infrastructure** — Automated rate engine with policy-based pricing
3. **Agent Banking Network** — National cash-in/cash-out network
4. **Merchant Acquiring** — QR + Payment Links + POS for all business sizes
5. **Payroll Infrastructure** — Digital salary distribution for enterprises
6. **Government Collections** — Tax, fees, utilities, social payments
7. **Remittance Corridors** — Inbound diaspora money transfer
8. **Savings & Financing** — Goal-based savings, Sharia-compliant lending
9. **Cards Infrastructure** — Virtual + physical prepaid cards
10. **Open Finance Layer** — API ecosystem for third-party innovation

## Market Context (Syria 2026)
- **Population**: ~22M (including diaspora)
- **Cash economy**: 85%+ of transactions
- **Banked population**: < 25%
- **Smartphone penetration**: ~60% (growing)
- **Internet coverage**: ~55% (concentrated urban)
- **Diaspora remittances**: $2-3B/year through informal channels
- **Unbanked SMEs**: 95%+ have no access to credit
- **Youth (under 25)**: 50%+ of population — mobile-first generation
