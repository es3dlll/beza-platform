# FX Engine User Personas

## Persona 1: Ahmad — Urban Professional (FX User)
```
Age: 28
Occupation: Software developer in Damascus
Income: 500,000 SYP/month
FX Need: Convert USD remittance from freelance work to SYP
Frequency: Monthly, $500-1,000
Pain points:
  - Exchange houses near his office offer different rates daily
  - No transparency on the rate he'll get until he walks in
  - Loses 3-5% on each conversion to exchange house spread
  - Receives payment in USD via wire transfer (slow, expensive)
Needs:
  - Real-time rate display in the app
  - Lock rate and convert instantly
  - Fair spread (target < 2%)
  - USD wallet to hold before conversion
```

## Persona 2: Khalid — Diaspora Remitter
```
Age: 35
Occupation: Taxi driver in Berlin
Income: €2,500/month
FX Need: Send €200-500/month to family in Damascus
Frequency: Monthly
Pain points:
  - Hawala charges 5-10% total cost (FX markup + fee)
  - Western Union charges €12 fee + 3-4% FX markup
  - No visibility on the actual rate used
  - Family receives in SYP at unknown rate
Needs:
  - Send EUR → SYP conversion in-app
  - Competitive rate (target < 3% all-in)
  - Rate lock so family knows exactly what they'll get
  - Delivery as USD or SYP to family's wallet
  - Recurring monthly transfer with auto-conversion
```

## Persona 3: Fatima — Small Business Owner (Merchant)
```
Age: 42
Occupation: Grocery store owner in Homs
Income: 1,000,000 SYP/month (irregular)
FX Need: Settle USD supplier invoices in SYP
Frequency: Weekly, $2,000-10,000
Pain points:
  - Suppliers quote in USD but she operates in SYP
  - Bank transfers take 3-5 days, rates change by then
  - Exchange houses don't handle large amounts (>$5K)
  - No ability to lock a rate for future settlement
Needs:
  - Rate lock for 24-48h to plan supplier payments
  - Large conversion capacity (up to $50K)
  - Settlement in supplier's USD wallet or bank account
  - Conversion history and receipts for accounting
  - Better rates for higher volume (>0.5% discount)
```

## Persona 4: Hadi — Beza Treasury Operator (Admin)
```
Age: 30
Occupation: Treasury analyst at Beza
Tech Literacy: High
FX Need: Manage rate sources, monitor exposure, report to CBS
Frequency: Daily
Pain points:
  - Multiple rate sources updating at different frequencies
  - Manual spread adjustments across user tiers
  - CBS requires daily rate reporting with audit trail
  - Needs to detect rate manipulation or anomalies quickly
Needs:
  - Dashboard showing all rate sources and their health
  - One-click spread adjustment per pair per tier
  - Rate override with full audit trail (who, when, why)
  - CBS report generation (exportable PDF/CSV)
  - Anomaly detection alerts (Slack, email)
  - Provider SLA and performance metrics
```
