# Government Collections Feature Vision

## Elevator Pitch
Beza Government Collections transforms how Syrians pay government dues — taxes, vehicle registration, passport fees, court fees, municipality charges, university tuition, and civil registry fees — from frustrating, multi-day bureaucratic ordeals into 30-second mobile payments. One app, every government service, official receipts instantly.

## Problem Statement
- Syrian citizens spend hours — often full days — travelling to government offices and queuing to pay fees
- Cash-only payment culture creates security risks, corruption opportunities, and opaque record-keeping
- Each ministry operates its own siloed payment system with no central digital gateway
- Citizens in rural areas face disproportionate travel burden to reach payment centres
- No unified digital receipt system — paper receipts are lost, forged, or contested
- Government revenue leakage due to manual cash handling and reconciliation gaps
- Monthly reconciliation between payment agents and ministries is manual, slow, error-prone

## Target Users
- **Primary**: All Syrian citizens age 18+ who owe government fees (income tax, property tax, vehicle registration, passport, court, civil registry)
- **Secondary**: University students paying semester tuition (public and private universities)
- **Tertiary**: Businesses and institutions filing corporate taxes and municipal fees
- **Quaternary**: Syrian diaspora renewing passports or civil documents from abroad

## Core Capabilities
| Capability | Priority | Description |
|------------|----------|-------------|
| Tax query & pay | P0 | Query income/property tax by Tax ID, view amount due, pay instantly |
| Vehicle registration | P0 | Pay annual registration, licence renewal, ownership transfer fees |
| Passport fee payment | P0 | Pay passport issuance/renewal fees by application number |
| Court fee payment | P0 | Pay case filing fees, fines, appeal deposits |
| Municipality fees | P1 | Pay municipal services tax, waste collection, property fees |
| University tuition | P0 | Pay semester tuition for public and private universities |
| Civil registry fees | P1 | Pay certificate issuance (birth, marriage, death, ID renewal) |
| Traffic fine payment | P0 | Query and pay traffic fines by licence plate or fine number |
| Government receipt | P0 | Generate official government payment receipt with QR validation |
| Payment history | P0 | Full history of all government payments made through Beza |
| Reconciliation | P0 | Automated daily reconciliation with ministry systems |
| Multi-ministry support | P0 | Integration with Ministry of Finance, Interior, Higher Education, Justice, Municipalities |
| Bulk payment | P2 | Pay multiple government fees in a single transaction |
| Corporate filing | P2 | Business tax filing and payment integration |
