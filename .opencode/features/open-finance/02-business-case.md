# Open Finance Business Case

## Revenue Model
| Revenue Stream | Calculation Model | Margin | Y3 Annual Revenue |
|---------------|-------------------|--------|-------------------|
| Transaction Fee (API) | 0.2% per API-initiated txn (cap 2,000 SYP) | 90% | $800K |
| Subscription (Startup) | $50/month — 10K API calls/day | 95% | $300K |
| Subscription (Business) | $200/month — 100K API calls/day | 95% | $600K |
| Subscription (Enterprise) | $1,000/month — unlimited | 95% | $500K |
| Overage (per 1K calls) | $0.50 per 1K beyond plan | 98% | $200K |
| Premium Support | $500/month dedicated support | 80% | $100K |
| Sandbox-to-Production fee | $500 onboarding fee | 90% | $50K |

## Unit Economics
| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| CAC (organic) | $50 |
