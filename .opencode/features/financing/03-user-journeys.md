# رحلات المستخدم — User Journeys

## Journey 1: Qard Hasan — Emergency Loan

### Persona
- **Name**: Layla
- **Age**: 32
- **Occupation**: Teacher
- **Scenario**: Unexpected hospital bill of SYP 300,000

### Steps
1. **Discover** — Layla sees "Qard Hasan" card on Beza home screen
2. **Apply** — Taps "Apply Now" → selects Qard Hasan → enters SYP 300,000 → picks 90-day term
3. **Guarantor** — System prompts to invite a guarantor → Layla selects her brother from contacts
4. **Brother receives notification** → approves guarantee with e-signature
5. **Credit Check** — System runs ML credit score on Layla's 8-month wallet history (score: 680/850)
6. **Instant Decision** — Application approved (scoring + guarantor confidence)
7. **e-Sign** — Layla reviews terms: Principal SYP 300,000, Admin fee SYP 3,000, 0% profit, due in 90 days
8. **Disbursement** — SYP 300,000 credited to Layla's wallet within 45 minutes
9. **Repayment** — Auto-deduct SYP 3,333/day for 90 days starting day 8
10. **Completion** — After 90 days, loan marked as completed → credit score improves +30 points

## Journey 2: Murabaha — Laptop for University

### Persona
- **Name**: Khaled
- **Age**: 20
- **Occupation**: University student
- **Scenario**: Needs laptop (SYP 2,500,000) for engineering studies

### Steps
1. **Browse** — Khaled opens Murabaha section → selects "Electronics" → browses laptop catalog
2. **Select Item** — Chooses specific laptop → price displayed: SYP 2,500,000 (cost) + 8% profit (SYP 200,000) = SYP 2,700,000 total
3. **Customize Term** — Selects 12-month installment plan → monthly payment: SYP 225,000
4. **Apply** — Submits application with university enrollment proof
5. **Verification** — Beza verifies student status and checks credit score (score: 620/850 — adequate)
6. **Approval** — Approved with 10% down payment (SYP 250,000 from Khaled's wallet)
7. **Purchase** — Beza purchases laptop from approved supplier (order confirmed)
8. **Delivery** — Laptop delivered to Khaled via Beza logistics tracking
9. **Contract** — Khaled e-signs Murabaha contract: Cost SYP 2,500,000, Profit SYP 200,000, Total SYP 2,700,000
10. **Installments** — Monthly auto-deduction of SYP 225,000 from wallet on 1st of each month
11. **Ownership** — After 12th payment, full ownership transferred to Khaled

## Journey 3: Micro-Enterprise — Inventory Restocking

### Persona
- **Name**: Abu Sami
- **Age**: 45
- **Occupation**: Small grocery store owner (Beza Agent)
- **Scenario**: Needs SYP 5,000,000 to restock inventory before Eid

### Steps
1. **Eligibility Check** — Abu Sami sees "Micro-Enterprise" offer on Agent dashboard
2. **Pre-qualify** — System auto-analyzes 14 months of wallet transaction data → pre-approved for SYP 4,000,000
3. **Full Application** — Applies for SYP 5,000,000 → uploads business registration, 3 months supplier invoices
4. **Cash Flow Analysis** — ML model analyzes daily sales data from wallet → confirms capacity
5. **Credit Committee** — Manual review (automated for amounts > SYP 3,000,000) approves
6. **Contract** — Signed as Murabaha for inventory: Cost SYP 5,000,000, Profit 10% = SYP 500,000
7. **Disbursement** — Funds paid directly to suppliers (not to Abu Sami's wallet)
8. **Repayment** — Flexible schedule: Min SYP 200,000/month, auto-deduct from daily agent commissions
9. **Monitoring** — Quarterly cash flow review; if revenue dips, restructuring available
10. **Completion** — After full repayment, Abu Sami eligible for SYP 10,000,000 next cycle

## Journey 4: Default & Collection

### Persona
- **Name**: Nadia
- **Age**: 28
- **Occupation**: Freelance designer
- **Scenario**: Lost client contract → unable to pay Murabaha installment

### Timeline
1. **Day 0** — Missed payment → auto-retry (3 attempts over 48 hours)
2. **Day 3** — SMS + in-app notification reminder
3. **Day 7** — Soft call from Beza support → offers restructuring
4. **Day 14** — Nadia accepts restructuring: remaining balance extended by 3 months
5. **Day 30** — Restructured payment made → account back to active
6. **Charity Fee** — Late penalties (SYP 10,000/day × 14 days = SYP 140,000) moved to charity account

If no response:
7. **Day 30** — Escalation to collection team
8. **Day 45** — Guarantor contacted
9. **Day 60** — Legal notice (if amount > SYP 1,000,000)
10. **Day 90+** — Provisioned as bad debt; referred to external collection (Sharia-compliant)
