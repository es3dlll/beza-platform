# Financing Feature — Index

## Overview
This feature bible documents the complete **Sharia-compliant financing** module for the Beza Platform. It covers three Islamic financing products, their end-to-end workflows, credit scoring, disbursement, repayment, and collection.

## Product Catalog
| # | Product | Type | Arabic Name |
|---|---------|------|-------------|
| 1 | Qard Hasan | 0% interest, short-term | قرض حسن |
| 2 | Murabaha | Cost-plus financing | مرابحة |
| 3 | Micro-Enterprise Financing | Business cash-flow based | تمويل المنشآت الصغيرة |
