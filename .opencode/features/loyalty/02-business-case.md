# Loyalty Business Case

## Revenue Model
| Revenue Stream | Calculation Model | Margin | Y3 Annual Revenue |
|---------------|-------------------|--------|-------------------|
| Points Liability Float | Unredeemed points = deferred revenue (3% float income) | 100% | $500K |
| Merchant Campaign Fees | $50/month per campaign + 2% of campaign rewards | 80% | $600K |
| Partner Commission | 10% commission on partner reward redemptions | 90% | $300K |
| Tier Upgrade Upsell | Premium tier benefits drive higher transaction volume | Indirect | $1M |
| Data Insights (B2B) | Aggregated spending patterns for merchants | 95% | $200K |

## Cost Structure (Monthly)
| Cost Item | Monthly (USD) | Annual (USD) |
|-----------|--------------|--------------|
| Points liability accrual (0.5% of TP) | $50K | $600K |
| Reward procurement (airtime, gift cards) | $20K | $240K |
| Infrastructure | $10K | $120K |
| Staff (5 people) | $35K | $420K |
| Partner management | $10K | $120K |
| **Total** | **$125K** | **$1.5M** |

## Unit Economics
| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Points earned per active user/month | 500 pts |
| Points redeemed per active user/month | 200 pts |
| Cost per 1,000 points (liability) | $0.50 |
| Revenue per 1,000 points earned | $0.05 (float) |
| Revenue per active user/month (indirect) | $1.20 |
| LTV uplift from loyalty (24mo) | $15 |

## Break-even Analysis
| Year | Users | Revenue | Cost | Profit/Loss |
