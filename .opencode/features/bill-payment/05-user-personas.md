# Bill Payment User Personas

## Persona 1: Mahmoud — Urban Professional
```
Age: 34
Occupation: Engineer at a private company in Damascus
Income: 600,000 SYP/month
Tech literacy: High
Smartphone: Yes (Samsung Galaxy S22)
Bills paid monthly:
  - Electricity (PEED): 35,000–55,000 SYP
  - Water (Damascus Water): 8,000–15,000 SYP
  - Mobile (Syriatel): 25,000 SYP (postpaid + data)
  - Internet (Aya): 30,000 SYP
  - Landline (Syria Telecom): 5,000 SYP
  - Total monthly: 103,000–130,000 SYP

Pain points:
  - Spends 3 hours/month queuing at 5 different service centers
  - Forgets due dates → pays 5% late penalty on electricity (1,750–2,750 SYP)
  - Syriatel cuts service if unpaid by 15th each month
  - Carries 130,000 SYP cash for bills (unsafe)
  - No consolidated record of paid bills

Needs:
  - Pay all bills from one app
  - Reminders before due dates
  - Digital receipts for expense tracking
  - Auto-pay for fixed-amount bills (internet, water)
```

## Persona 2: Layla — Diaspora Daughter
```
Age: 29
Occupation: Accountant in Dubai
Income: 8,000 AED/month
Tech literacy: High
Smartphone: Yes (iPhone 14)

Responsible for family bills in Damascus:
  - Mother's electricity (PEED): 25,000–40,000 SYP
  - Family landline (Syria Telecom): 5,000 SYP
  - Brother's MTN mobile: 15,000 SYP
  - Total monthly: 45,000–60,000 SYP

Pain points:
  - Sends extra money to mother for bills via hawala (7% fee)
  - Mother must walk to service centers (she's 62)
  - Bills get lost or delayed → late penalties
  - No way to verify bills were paid
  - Exchange rate uncertainty when sending cash

Needs:
  - Pay family bills remotely from Dubai
  - Fetch bill amounts directly (no manual entry)
  - Receipts sent to her and to mother's phone
  - Scheduled payment for recurring bills
  - SYP payment from Beza wallet (funded via remittance)
```

## Persona 3: Abu Khaled — Small Business Owner
```
Age: 48
Occupation: Bakery owner in Barzeh, Damascus
Income: 2,000,000 SYP/month (variable)
Tech literacy: Low-Medium
Smartphone: Yes (budget Android)

Commercial bills:
  - Electricity (PEED commercial): 120,000–250,000 SYP
  - Water (Damascus Water): 25,000–40,000 SYP
  - MTN business line: 35,000 SYP
  - Syria Telecom office line: 8,000 SYP
  - Total monthly: 188,000–333,000 SYP

Pain points:
  - High electricity bills (commercial rate) must be paid by 10th or cut
  - Cannot leave bakery for 2 hours to queue at PEED
  - Sends employee with cash (theft risk)
  - No digital record for accountant
  - Business expense tracking is manual/envelope-based

Needs:
  - Pay high-value commercial bills (200K+ SYP)
  - Transaction receipts for accounting
  - Schedule payments before due dates
  - Multiple bills at once (electricity + water + telecom)
  - Payment confirmation to share with accountant
```
