# Savings Product Strategy

## Product Philosophy
"Save a little, achieve a lot — together."

Savings should be effortless, social, and rewarding. Beza Savings turns good intentions into automatic habits. Every deposit earns a share of profit (not interest), keeping the product pure, transparent, and accessible.

## Release Phases

### Phase 1 — Foundation (MVP)
- Single currency (SYP) goal creation with custom name, target, date
- Manual deposits from main wallet
- Basic progress tracking (progress bar, percentage)
- Goal completion and withdrawal
- Individual goals only

### Phase 2 — Automation & Smart Features
- Auto-save: daily or weekly recurring deposits
- Round-up spare change from wallet transactions
- Goal lock period (savings released only at target date)
- Early withdrawal with penalty fee
- Push notification nudges and milestone celebrations

### Phase 3 — Profit Sharing
- Pooled savings investment in Sharia-compliant instruments
- Periodic profit calculation and distribution
- Transparent profit reporting per goal
- Qard Hasan principle: principal guaranteed, profit variable

### Phase 4 — Social & Team
- Team goals: invite members to save together
- Contribution tracking per member
- Team milestone celebrations
- Public/private goal visibility options
- Jameeya-style fixed contribution schedules

### Phase 5 — Advanced
- Multi-currency goals (SYP + USD)
- Goal templates (Education, Wedding, Phone, Hajj)
- Savings challenges (30-day challenge, Ramadan special)
- Savings insights and recommendations
- Goal-linked cards (spend from goal directly)

| Team/group | ✅ Invite + track | ❌ | ✅ (but informal) |
