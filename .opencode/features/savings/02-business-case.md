# Savings Business Case

## Revenue Model
| Revenue Stream | Calculation | Margin | Y3 Annual Revenue |
|---------------|-------------|--------|-------------------|
| Float income on savings pool | 2–3% return on pooled deposits invested in low-risk Sharia-compliant instruments | 100% | $2M |
| Early withdrawal penalty | 2% of withdrawn amount if goal is locked | 100% | $200K |
| Round-up rounding gain | 0–999 SYP per txn retained as operational income when exact round-up not possible | 100% | $150K |
| Premium savings features | 2,500 SYP/month for unlimited goals + team goals | 90% | $300K |
| Profit share management fee | 10% of profits distributed | 100% | $100K |

## Cost Structure (Monthly)
| Cost Item | Monthly (USD) | Annual (USD) |
|-----------|--------------|--------------|
| CFE sub-account provisioning | $5K | $60K |
| Scheduled job infrastructure (auto-save, round-up) | $3K | $36K |
| Profit share calculation engine | $2K | $24K |
| Push notification costs (goal nudges) | $1K | $12K |
| Compliance review (Sharia board) | $5K | $60K |
| **Total** | **$16K** | **$192K** |

## Unit Economics
| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| CAC (savings feature activation) | $0.50 |
| Average savings balance per user | 150,000 SYP ($30) |
| Average revenue per savings user | $0.15/month |
| Payback period | 3–4 months |
| LTV (24 months) | $3.60 |

## Break-even Analysis
| Year | Savings Users | Revenue | Cost | Profit/Loss |
|------|--------------|---------|------|-------------|
| 1 | 50K | $180K | $192K | ($12K) |
| 2 | 200K | $950K | $500K | $450K |
| 3 | 500K | $2.75M | $800K | $1.95M |

## Strategic Value
Beyond direct revenue, savings drives stickiness: users with active savings goals have 3x higher D90 retention and 2x higher average wallet balance. Savings float provides stable liquidity for Beza's financing products.
