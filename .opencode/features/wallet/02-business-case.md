# Wallet Business Case

## Revenue Model
| Revenue Stream | Calculation Model | Margin | Y3 Annual Revenue |
|---------------|-------------------|--------|-------------------|
| P2P Transfer Fee | 0.5% per txn (cap 5,000 SYP) | 90% | $3M |
| Agent Cash-in Fee | 1% of amount (paid by user) | 60% | $2M |
| Agent Cash-out Fee | 1.5% of amount (paid by user) | 60% | $3M |
| Bill Payment Commission | 0.5-2% per bill type | 100% | $1.5M |
| Airtime Commission | 3-5% margin | 80% | $1M |
| FX Spread (SYP/USD) | 1-3% spread | 100% | $2.5M |
| Card Issuance Fee | 5,000 SYP virtual, 15,000 SYP physical | 70% | $1M |
| Card Interchange | 0.5-1.5% per transaction | 80% | $1.5M |
| Savings Float Income | 3% on held deposits | 100% | $1M |
| Premium Account | 5,000 SYP/month (higher limits, priority support) | 90% | $500K |
