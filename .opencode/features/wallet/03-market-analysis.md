# Wallet Market Analysis

## Competitive Landscape (Syria 2026)

### Direct Competitors
| Competitor | Type | Market Share | Key Weakness |
|------------|------|-------------|--------------|
| Syriatel Cash | Mobile Money | 30% | Telecom-locked, limited features |
| MTN MoMo | Mobile Money | 25% | Same as Syriatel |
| Syria Mobile Pay | Mobile Wallet | 5% | Limited adoption |
| Informal Hawala | P2P Transfer | 40% (remittances) | Slow, expensive, unregulated |
| Cash | Physical | 85%+ daily txns | Inconvenient, unsafe |

### Indirect Competitors
| Competitor | Type | Threat Level |
|------------|------|-------------|
| Local banks (BBS, Bemo, etc.) | Traditional banking | Medium — low mobile adoption |
| Exchange houses (Al-Maghrib, etc.) | FX + Remittance | High — entrenched, trusted |
| Gold/jewelry stores | Store of value | Medium — cultural preference |

## Competitive Advantages
| Beza Advantage | Competitor Gap |
|---------------|---------------|
| Multi-currency wallet | All competitors are single-currency (SYP) |
| Super App (pay, save, borrow, invest) | Competitors are single-feature |
| Agent network integrated with digital | MoMo agents are telecom-only |
| Diaspora remittance corridors | Hawala is slow, banks are expensive |
| AI-powered fraud & credit | Competitors have basic/no fraud detection |
| Sharia-compliant by design | Only a few offer limited Islamic products |
| Modern UX (Revolut-level) | Competitors have basic, outdated interfaces |
