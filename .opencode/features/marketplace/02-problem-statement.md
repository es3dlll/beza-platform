# Problem Statement

## Current Pain Points

### 1. Fragmented Digital Purchasing
Users in Syria and the region must navigate multiple apps, websites, and physical stores to purchase digital goods. Mobile top-up requires a separate app or scratch card; gift cards require visiting merchant locations; internet packages involve complex vendor-specific portals. This fragmentation creates friction, wastes time, and reduces adoption of digital payments.

### 2. Limited Digital Payment Adoption
Cash remains dominant. Users lack trust in online payments due to fraud concerns and poor merchant reliability. Those who want to pay digitally often cannot find a single trusted platform that aggregates multiple services.

### 3. Mobile Top-Up Inefficiencies
- Scratch cards run out of stock at retailers
- Users must remember multiple USSD codes for different networks and bundles
- No centralized transaction history for top-ups
- Small-denomination top-ups incur disproportionate time cost

### 4. Gift Card Market Gap
No digital gift card marketplace exists for Syrian and regional merchants. Users must buy physical gift cards in person, limiting gifting occasions and cross-merchant flexibility.

### 5. Vendor Discovery Challenges
Small digital goods vendors lack a platform to reach customers. Users struggle to find reliable sellers for digital products like game credits, streaming subscriptions, and software licenses.

## Opportunity

Beza Marketplace solves all of the above by providing:

- A single, trusted digital storefront inside the Beza super-app
- Direct telecom integration for instant mobile top-up
- Digital gift card issuance and redemption
- Curated vendor marketplace with commission-based economics
- Unified wallet-based payments with fraud protection
- Rewards program incentivizing digital transactions

## Success Criteria

| Criterion | Measure |
|---|---|
| User adoption | 50,000+ monthly active buyers |
| Transaction volume | 750M+ SYP monthly GMV |
| Digital delivery reliability | > 99.5% success rate |
| User retention | 60%+ repeat purchase rate at 6 months |
| Vendor satisfaction | NPS > 50 among active vendors |
