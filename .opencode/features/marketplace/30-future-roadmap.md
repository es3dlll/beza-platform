# Future Roadmap

## v1.0 — MVP (Current)
- Mobile top-up (Syriatel + MTN)
- Digital goods (game credits, streaming codes)
- Wallet payment with hold/release
- Basic vendor onboarding (invite-only)
- Product catalog with search and categories

## v1.1 — Gift Cards (Q3 2026)
- Gift card purchase and send (5 merchants)
- WhatsApp/SMS delivery
- QR code generation for in-store redemption
- Merchant redemption portal
- Gift card balance checking
- Auto top-up feature
- Saved recipient numbers

## v1.2 — Bill Payment & Rewards (Q4 2026)
- Utility bill payment (electricity, water, internet)
- Telecom bill payment
- Rewards integration (loyalty points on marketplace purchases)
- Promo code engine v2
- Scheduled/delayed top-up
- Internet data bundles direct purchase
- Vendor bulk upload improvements

## v1.3 — Physical Goods & Scale (Q1 2027)
- Physical goods support
- Carrier tracking integration
- Open vendor registration (self-serve)
- Cash on delivery option
- Bulk gift card purchase (enterprise)
- Advanced vendor analytics
- Multi-currency support (USD, EUR for international products)

## v2.0 — Ecosystem (Q2-Q3 2027)
- Vendor mobile app (fulfillment on the go)
- AI-powered product recommendations
- Dynamic pricing engine
- Subscription marketplace (monthly boxes, recurring)
- Beza Pay QR (in-store marketplace payments)
- P2P gift card trading
- Escrow service for high-value transactions
- Marketplace API for third-party integrations

## v2.1 — Regional Expansion (Q4 2027)
- Lebanese telecom integration (Alfa, Touch)
- Iraqi telecom integration (Zain, Asiacell, Korek)
- Regional gift card merchants (UAE, KSA, Jordan)
- Multi-language support (Kurdish, Turkish)
- Cross-border gift card gifting
- Regional settlement (multi-currency)

## v2.2 — Advanced Features (2028)
- Crypto payment option (USDT)
- NFT-based digital receipts
- AI vendor assistant (auto-fulfillment, pricing suggestions)
- Social commerce (shared carts, group buying)
- Marketplace API public launch
- White-label marketplace for enterprise clients
- Real-time inventory sync with vendor ERP systems
