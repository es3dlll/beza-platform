# Compliance & Legal

## Regulatory Framework

### Syrian Telecommunications Law
- Beza must be a registered telecom service provider or partner with licensed operators
- All top-up transactions subject to Syrian Telecommunications Regulatory Authority (STRA) oversight
- Data privacy law: Personal data must be stored within Syria (or approved jurisdiction)
- Transaction records retained for minimum 7 years per Syrian commercial law

### E-Commerce Regulations
- Digital goods classified as "services" under Syrian e-commerce framework
- Vendor terms and conditions must be clearly displayed
- Right of withdrawal: Digital goods once delivered are non-refundable by default
- Cooling-off period: 24 hours for undelivered digital goods; 14 days for physical goods

### Anti-Money Laundering (AML)
- Transaction monitoring for suspicious patterns
- Single transaction limit: 500,000 SYP per order (pending regulatory approval)
- Daily transaction limit: 2,000,000 SYP per user
- KYC required for vendors (business license + owner ID)
- Vendors exceeding 5,000,000 SYP monthly volume require enhanced due diligence

## User Protection

### Refund Policy

| Scenario | Refund Policy | Processing Time |
|---|---|---|
| Top-up failed (API error) | Full auto-refund | Immediate (< 5 min) |
| Digital code not delivered | Full refund after 30 min | < 1 hour |
| Digital code invalid/expired | Vendor must replace or refund | < 24 hours |
| Gift card unredeemed (within 14 days) | Full refund (minus 5% fee) | < 48 hours |
| Physical goods damaged | Vendor resolves or refund | < 7 days |
| User cancels before fulfillment | Full refund | Immediate |
| Wrong product delivered | Vendor replaces or full refund | < 48 hours |

### Dispute Resolution
1. User submits dispute via order detail page
2. Vendor has 24 hours to respond
3. If unresolved, Beza admin reviews and makes final decision
4. Escalation: Beza customer support (48h max resolution)
5. Final escalation: STRA consumer protection (for telecom-related)

## Data Privacy

- PII access: Only authorized personnel with business justification
- Data retention: Transaction data 7 years, PII marketing data 2 years
- User right to deletion: Core transaction data exempt (legal retention); marketing data deleted on request
- Cross-border transfer: Telecom data shared only with licensed operators within Syria
- Vendor data: Business license and owner ID stored encrypted; retained 5 years after vendor deactivation
