# 01 — Product Vision

## Elevator Pitch

**Beza Payroll** enables Syrian enterprises to replace cash salary payments with instant, compliant digital disbursements. Companies upload a salary sheet (CSV or API) → Beza debits the company's on-platform account → credits every employee's Beza wallet in bulk → each employee receives an instant notification with a downloadable payslip. No bank accounts required. No cash logistics. Full audit trail.

## Vision Statement

> *"Every Syrian employee receives their salary digitally, on time, every time — without touching cash."*

## Core Problem

- 70 %+ of Syrian enterprises pay salaries in cash
- Cash payroll carries security risks (theft, loss, counterfeit)
- Manual distribution is slow (2–5 days for 100+ employees)
- No digital audit trail for labour disputes or tax reporting
- Employees in remote areas have no access to banking

## Beza Solution

| Problem | Beza Payroll Solution |
|---------|----------------------|
| Cash logistics | Bulk wallet credits — instant, traceable |
| No bank access | Beza Wallet (mobile-first, no minimum balance) |
| Slow distribution | Batch processing < 5 seconds per 1,000 employees |
| No records | Auto-generated PDF payslips + full API audit log |
| Fraud / theft | Multi-signature approval, 3D secure company auth |

## Success Metrics

- **Adoption:** 500+ companies enrolled within 12 months
- **Volume:** SYP 50 Bn+ monthly payroll volume
- **Speed:** 99.9 % of batches processed within 60 seconds
- **Reliability:** 99.5 % first-attempt success rate
- **Satisfaction:** NPS > 60 (company HR); NPS > 70 (employees)

## Target Market

- **Primary:** Syrian private sector — SMEs (10–500 employees)
- **Secondary:** NGOs, schools, factories, retail chains
- **Tertiary:** Government agencies (Phase 3)
