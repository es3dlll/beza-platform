# 01 — Feature Vision & Strategy

## 1.1 Vision Statement

Beza Education empowers every Syrian family to pay for schooling — from kindergarten through university — through a single, trusted digital platform. Schools digitise fee collection, reduce cash dependency, and gain real-time visibility into payments.

## 1.2 Why Education

- **80 % of Syrian schools still operate on cash** — parents carry bundles of SYP, schools manually reconcile.
- **Private education is growing** despite economic pressure; families prioritise children's future.
- **Hyperinflation (100-200 % annually)** means fees change termly — digital tracking is essential.
- **Remittance families** need to pay school fees from abroad (sister feature: Remittance Hub).
- **Banking penetration is low** (~30 %) but mobile wallet adoption is accelerating.

## 1.3 Strategic Alignment

| Beza Platform Goal | Education Contribution |
|---|---|
| Increase MAU | Parents open Beza monthly per term/semester. Schools onboard thousands of families. |
| Grow TPV | Education fees are high-ticket: 500K-5M SYP per student per year. |
| Cross-sell | Wallet, Remittance, Bill Payment, Financing (fee instalments). |
| Merchant network | Schools become Beza merchants; staff payroll via Payroll Platform. |
| Data moat | Payment history → credit scoring for parents, financing products. |

## 1.4 Target Segments

1. **K-12 Private Schools** — 800+ schools in Syria, avg 400 students, 2-8 M SYP annual fee.
2. **K-12 Public Schools** — 14,000+ schools, lower fees (50-150 K SYP), high volume.
3. **Private Universities** — 25+ licensed, avg 5000 students, 1.5-5 M SYP annual tuition.
4. **Public Universities** — 4 main (Damascus, Aleppo, Tishreen, Baath), 200 K+ students.
5. **Tutoring Centres** — 3,000+ across Syria, monthly subscriptions.
6. **Online Course Providers** — emerging segment post-2020.

## 1.5 Key Metrics (Year 1)

- **Schools onboarded**: 100
- **Students registered**: 50,000
- **Parents active**: 40,000
- **Total transactions**: 200,000+
- **TPV**: 50 B SYP (~$2M at parallel rate)
- **Revenue**: 1.25 B SYP (2.5 % take rate)
