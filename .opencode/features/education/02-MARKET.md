## 2.2 Current Payment Behaviour

| Method | Share | Issues |
|---|---|---|
| Cash at school | 70 % | Theft, counterfeit, queues, reconciliation nightmare |
| Bank transfer | 15 % | Only urban, slow, requires bank account |
| Western Union/MoneyGram | 8 % | Fees, queues, diaspora-to-school friction |
| Mobile wallet | 5 % | Very nascent, no dedicated education product |
| Cheque | 2 % | Declining, trust issues |
