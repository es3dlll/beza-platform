# Executive Summary

## Strategic Context
The Syrian crisis remains the largest displacement crisis globally, with **13.4 million people** in need of humanitarian assistance and **6.8 million internally displaced persons (IDPs)** as of 2026. Traditional in-kind aid distribution faces severe challenges: logistics bottlenecks, security risks, leakage, and lack of beneficiary dignity. Digital cash-based assistance offers a scalable, dignified, and auditable alternative.

## Beza Humanitarian Solution
Beza's Humanitarian Aid feature enables NGOs, UN agencies (WFP, UNICEF, UNHCR), and charitable organizations to:
- **Distribute Multi-Purpose Cash (MPC)** directly to beneficiary wallets
- **Issue conditional cash transfers (CCT)** for specific needs (health, education, shelter)
- **Deploy e-voucher programs** for restricted items at partner merchants
- **Track spending patterns** for donor compliance and outcome reporting
- **Verify beneficiaries biometrically** at distribution points via agent network

## Supported Programs
1. **Multi-Purpose Cash (MPC)** — Unconditional cash, 75 USD/month equivalent per household
2. **Conditional Cash for Education** — 30 USD/month per child attending school
3. **Winterization Voucher** — 120 USD one-time for heating fuel, blankets, winter clothing
4. **Food Voucher** — 45 USD/month for staple foods at partner merchants
5. **Health Cash** — Coverage of medical expenses up to 200 USD per episode
